> **Sample data.** Two fictional brands (GlowLeaf, LumaDerm), 20 fictional creators, synthetic metrics.

## What it does

Once an influencer campaign goes live, an account manager has to notice what went wrong across dozens of creators: posts that never went up, missing `#ad` disclosure, underperformance against the promised reach, suspicious engagement, a negative comment spike, or a post deleted early. Post-Live Watch screens the campaign data, raises flags in a queue sorted into Act now / Needs a look / OK, and drafts the nudge message for the manager to send.

## How it works

1. **Ingest metrics** — seeded demo data, manual entry, or a gated Instagram connector with two Meta API tiers (tested against recorded fixtures; no Meta app exists yet).
2. **Resolve the deal terms** — real creator deals negotiate things away from the brief (a waived hashtag, a barter deal, no reach guarantee). Overrides are resolved in one place before any detector runs, and the card explains why a requirement doesn't apply.
3. **Detect** — 12 rules: not posted, posted late, missing disclosure, missing tag/link/hashtag, underperforming, suspicious engagement, negative sentiment spike, deleted early, off-brief caption edits, expired Story without proof, competitor post, and viral winner.
4. **Explain** — AI adds language on top: why a post underperformed, Hinglish and sarcasm sentiment, paraphrase detection, and a drafted nudge.
5. **Decide** — approve, edit, or dismiss; every decision lands in a decision log.

New deals and whole campaigns can be entered from an account manager's shorthand note: AI drafts, code validates, and nothing is written until the manager confirms. Disclosure can never be waived, and the AI is forbidden from inventing a creator's reach.

## Design choices

- **Rules first, AI on top.** Every flag comes from a transparent rule reading one editable `config/thresholds.yaml`. Turn AI off and the app still works end to end on keywords and templates.
- **Correctness before UI.** The rules engine was built and verified with `pytest` before any UI code; 180 tests are the correctness proof.
- **No score without evidence.** A creator with no history gets "New — no track record yet", and a missing reach figure is never scored as zero.
- **$0 to run.** React on Vercel, FastAPI + SQLite on Render's free plan, a GitHub Actions keep-alive, and free OpenRouter models only, with a hard `max_price` of 0 on every request.
